# Jyothiesh Business – Internal Policies

The company follows an internal project management system called JTrack.

Every new project receives a unique identification code beginning with JTB.

The company conducts employee performance reviews every 75 days.

Customer support operates between 10 AM and 7 PM, Monday through Saturday.

The JyoBot product has a maximum of 15,000 customer interactions per month under its standard subscription.

The company provides a 12% discount to customers purchasing two or more products together.

All project expenses above ₹35,000 require approval from the operations manager.

The company's internal cloud infrastructure is named JyoCloud.

Database backups are performed every 18 hours.

The company plans to introduce a fourth product called JyoFlow in July 2027, with a proposed subscription price of ₹1,099 per month. The launch has not yet been officially approved.
